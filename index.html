LLM reliabilityDeployed serviceFeb–Jun 2026

Reliable Korean YouTube misinformation detection

An LLM system that returns a verdict only when it is reliable enough, and abstains otherwise. Built for Ewha’s Challenge Semester and awarded the Outstanding Project scholarship.

Transcripts
1,435
Accepted accuracy
79.3% to 88.8%
Reasoning runs per video
7
  • Ran Qwen3.5-4B seven times per video with JSON-structured outputs, then scored how consistent its reasoning was with TF-IDF similarity and answer entropy.
  • Used conformal prediction to turn those signals into accept or abstain decisions, validated across randomized splits.
  • Shipped it as a web service (React, FastAPI, Vercel, RunPod) and ran a 30-user evaluation.

Code on GitHub Frontend demo

The demo shows the interface. Live GPU inference is paused to save compute cost.

2026 LLM + GBDT

ConFuse: context-aware fusion for sleep prediction

ETRI Human Understanding AI Challenge. Paper presented at ICTC 2026, co-first author

Combines a fine-tuned Qwen2.5-7B-Instruct with LightGBM, XGBoost, and CatBoost. A sample-wise, reliability-guided weight decides how much to trust the LLM based on data quality and available context.

0.6415 mean binary log loss

2026 KIST

Do LLMs answer the same in Korean and English?

Cross-lingual reliability study, KIST Brain Science Institute

Gave 9 LLMs five psychological scales (106 items) in both languages and compared response distributions, variability, entropy, item-level shifts, and scale reliability, looking at patterns per model instead of averages alone.

2026 KIST

Neuron-astrocyte Transformer

Biologically grounded attention research, KIST Brain Science Institute

Extended a neuron-astrocyte formulation of attention from one head to multi-head attention, derived when it matches standard MHA, and compared both in PyTorch with lesion and control experiments.

When sequence length seemed to drive the results, I added a control run and dropped the interpretations that didn’t hold up.

2025 Applied ML

Diabetes risk from diet and lifestyle

Ewha Data Science Lab, team lead

Joined health-exam and 24-hour dietary records for 4,358 KNHANES respondents, compared logistic regression, random forest, and XGBoost with SMOTE, and explained the drivers with SHAP.

0.891 macro ROC-AUC
